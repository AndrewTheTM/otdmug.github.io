The Ohio Travel Demand Model Users Group was founded and is supported by the Ohio Department of Transportation.  Members include DOT Staff, Ohio MPO Staff, and consultants practicing in and around the state of Ohio.  The group is managed by a four-member board of directors that uphold the OTDMUG Bylaws.
