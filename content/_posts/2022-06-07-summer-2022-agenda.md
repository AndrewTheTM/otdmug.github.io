Riffe Center Information

Enter the Riffe Center through the doors on High Street (corner of High and State) and check in at theinformation desk straight back to the right to get a sticker badge. You cannot enter 
Riffe Center fromState Street; that is an employee only entrance.

Go through the security checkpoint, which requires a metal detector. All bags, phones, keys, &amp;c. will gothrough an x-ray scanner. Take the escalators up (2 sets) to the elevator lobby (you can avoid these byinquiring at the desk if you have a mobility constraint). Take the elevators to the 31st Floor. Your floor isselected at the elevator call screen; there are no buttons in the elevator. Conference Room South B willbe on the south side of the building to the right of the elevator lobby

## Parking

Parking is at the Riffe Center garage, entrance is off Front St. or you can park under the State House(access via WB East Broad St. or SB 3rd St.). There is a tunnel under High Street from this garage (GreenLevel, level -1) to the Huntington Center (directions below).

## Directions from the State House Underground Parking to the Riffe Center

Go to the center western staircase and go to the Green Level (Level -1). This will connect with a tunnelthat will take you under High Street. The tunnel from the Statehouse enters into an underground hallwaywith a couple shops in it. You take the hall south (left) past the gift shop and enter the Riffe Centerthrough a revolving door. Take the stairs on the left to the street level and check in at the informationdesk as described above. Alternately, you can take the stairs to street level, cross at one of theintersections, and enter the Riffe Center through the doors on High Street.
